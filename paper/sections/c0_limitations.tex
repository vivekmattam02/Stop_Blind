\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Development split only.}
All simulator results come from a frozen set of 61 \texttt{val\_seen} episodes, a development split
whose buildings appear in training. They support exact comparisons within this protocol.
They do not support a leaderboard claim or a claim about unseen buildings (\texttt{val\_unseen}).

\paragraph{Other remedies.}
We test only one remedy, inverse-frequency class weighting. We do not test the inflection weighting
of the VLN-CE baseline \citep{wijmans2019eqa}, which upweights changes of action, a dedicated
stopping head \citep{xiang2020l2stop}, or data rebalancing \citep{cheng2025navila}.

\paragraph{Statistical power.}
Under the preregistered design, a checkpoint with a true success rate of $10\%$ would have been
confirmed with probability about $0.52$, and one at $15\%$ with probability about $0.91$ (with the
other candidates at $2\%$ and every comparator at $0$). The sweep therefore cannot rule out modest
gains over the selected checkpoint. Each trained checkpoint's $0/61$ is consistent with a success
rate of up to $10.3\%$ when all 37 checkpoints are bounded together.

\paragraph{No working policy to compare.}
Every trained policy here fails in the simulator, so the per-class checks are observed only on
failing policies. How often they would flag a policy that navigates, and whether passing them
predicts success, is untested.

\paragraph{Three seeds and one configuration.}
Each treatment has three paired seeds, with one learning rate, training length, model, history
length and data split. Three seeds are below the count at which interval estimates over runs are
certified \citep{agarwal2021rliable}, so we report per-seed values beside every aggregate and run
no significance test across seeds.

\paragraph{Causal mechanism.}
Action frequency, offline per-class recall and the response to weighting are consistent with one
another. Frequency, however, also varies with what each action means and with the states in which
it occurs, so the results show what this procedure selected without establishing why.
